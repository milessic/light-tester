A steady setup, a reliable test

  1. Open this app over HTTPS on your iPhone and allow camera access.
  2. With the bicycle light off, let the preview settle and capture ambient brightness.
  3. Switch the light on, wait for the brightness to settle, then start. Timing begins at your tap.
  4. Leave this page open. After a sustained brightness drop, the timer stops, an alert sounds, and the result is saved.

This measures visible light duration, not lumens. Changes in room lighting, camera exposure, or movement affect detection. Leaving the page or losing the camera interrupts the test.
